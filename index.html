Why BigChange

You're not just replacing Clik. You're deciding what you never have to replace again.

Clik has been moulded around exactly how Green Man works — that's exactly why it's hard to leave. But it's ageing, it's server-based, and Joblogic's "2027 switch-off" claim doesn't match what Clik themselves are telling you (roughly 20% of customers migrated so far). Whatever the real timeline, a server upgrade is already due, and a cloud-based platform that grows with the business — rather than something you'll be evaluating again in three years — is the safer long-term call.
